"We do not chase trends. We chase a single feeling, precisely."

Noir Absolu began in a rented perfumer's studio in the 11th arrondissement, where founder and nose Élise Vasseur set herself one rule: every fragrance had to be traceable to a real place, a real hour of the day, a real season.

A decade on, the house still works this way — ten fragrances total, each aged for months before release, each one blended by hand in batches small enough that Élise still signs the first bottle of every run.

We believe restraint is a luxury. So we make less, and we make it properly.

How it's made

Every bottle passes through three hands before it reaches yours.

I.

The Composition

Élise builds each formula from raw absolutes and essential oils, often revising a blend for over a year before she's satisfied.

II.

The Maceration

Once blended, each batch rests in dark glass for six to ten weeks, allowing the notes to fully marry before bottling.

III.

The Bottle

Bottled and hand-labelled in Paris, in glass designed to be refilled rather than replaced.
